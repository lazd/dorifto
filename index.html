Lap recording

Recording captures your launch and shows once you cross the start/finish line. From the second lap, a ghost car drives your fastest (or previous) lap of the run. Press the record button to stop: the course resets, the laps are saved, and the replay plays. Before crossing the line, press play to watch saved laps. Replays show each lap's total time, with a 🏆 for the fastest lap of the run; use the arrows to skip to the previous or next lap. Resetting the car discards an unsaved run, but completed record laps are saved automatically for each map and direction. Choose Record lap in the ghost setting to race the all-time record from your first lap. Beating it replaces the ghost with your new record.
